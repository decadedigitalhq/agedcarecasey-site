For care providers in Casey & Cardinia

Aged Care Casey is an independent local guide for families. We're exploring two things with providers: fair, neutral referrals, and practical software for care teams.

Where we are, plainly: Aged Care Casey is an early-stage project. We are not currently a registered aged care provider and have no confirmed partnerships. Nothing on this page is an endorsement of, or by, any provider.

1. Local referrals

Families contact us asking who delivers care near them. With their explicit consent, we can pass an enquiry to providers who service their suburb and care type.

How referrals work, and the rules we hold ourselves to:
  • Families give separate consent before we share their details with anyone.
  • We don't charge or accept commissions for referrals into government-funded care.
  • We don't rank providers by who pays us. Any commercial relationship will be disclosed.
  • We never use words like "vetted" or "approved". We can only tell families what a provider has told us and what is publicly listed, and we ask them to check the provider's registration themselves.

2. Tools for care teams

We're exploring simple, modular software for the care sector, such as client and referral tracking, and reporting that saves staff time. This is at the discovery stage. We want to hear what's painful in your day first, before building anything.
