Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Aged Care Casey is an independent local information and referral service. We are not a government service and not yet a registered aged care provider. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you use a form on this site — the waitlist enquiry, the free guide download, the Support at Home fee estimator, or the workforce interest form — we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number (if provided)
  • Your suburb or general location
  • The type of care support you're enquiring about
  • Answers you give to the fee estimator or eligibility checker (e.g. pension status, care needs)
  • For the workforce form: languages spoken and care experience

We do not collect payment information, government ID numbers, or health/medical records through this site.

Why we collect it — and your two separate choices

On the main enquiry form, we ask for two separate, explicit permissions, because they are genuinely different things:

  • Referral consent (required to submit the form): permission to share your enquiry details with a local aged care provider who may be able to help you, so they can contact you directly.
  • Marketing consent (optional, unticked by default): permission for us to email you guides and updates in the future. You do not need to agree to this to get help with your enquiry.

We will never bundle these two things together, and ticking one does not automatically tick the other.

Who we share your information with

We only share your enquiry details with a local aged care provider when you have given referral consent and it's relevant to helping you find care. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising purposes.

Right now, we have not yet formalised confirmed partnerships with named providers — where we do refer you, we will always tell you who you're being connected with.

Where your data is stored

Form submissions on this site are processed using Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, and forwarded to our own email inbox. Formspree's own privacy practices apply to how they handle data in transit — you can read their policy at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry records for as long as reasonably needed to help with your enquiry and to understand demand for local aged care services in Casey and Cardinia. If you'd like your information deleted, contact us using the details below and we will action this promptly.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate information
  • Delete your information from our records
  • Withdraw marketing consent (referral consent, once acted on for a specific enquiry, can't be "un-shared" retroactively — but you can ask us not to make any further referrals)

Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages are useful. We do not use invasive tracking or sell browsing data to third parties.

Changes to this policy

As Aged Care Casey grows — including if we complete aged care provider registration and begin delivering services directly — this policy will be updated to reflect that, and the update date at the top of this page will change accordingly.
