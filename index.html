Who we are, right now
A straight answer, from someone
who lives here

Aged Care Casey exists because the system is genuinely confusing — and Casey and Cardinia, despite being Victoria's largest and fastest-growing council area, have some of the thinnest aged care provider coverage in the state. Families here are often left figuring out the new Support at Home program on their own.

We're a Casey-based team, and we want to be upfront: we are not yet a registered aged care provider. We're currently going through the process of becoming one. Until that's complete, our role is to help you understand the system and connect you with trusted local providers we've vetted — at no cost, and with no obligation.

Every enquiry we receive helps us understand what Casey and Cardinia families actually need most. That's genuinely useful to us as we build toward delivering services ourselves — and it means the help we point you toward today is based on real local knowledge, not a directory search.

We'll update this page the moment our own registration is complete and we can begin delivering services directly.
